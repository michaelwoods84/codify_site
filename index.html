About the author

Built by someone who actually works with this stuff.

I'm a quantitative analyst at a tier 1 investment bank. I work with code, data and financial models every day, and I built Codifyit to share that with people who don't.

As a parent, I found most explanations of what children learn were either too technical or too vague. At work, I saw talented colleagues held back by tools nobody had explained to them properly.

The goal isn't to turn you into a programmer. It's to explain the technical ideas that increasingly affect your work and your family, clearly and practically.
