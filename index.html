About the author

Written by someone who works with code, data and AI every day.

I'm a quantitative analyst at a tier 1 investment bank. I work with code, data and financial models every day, and I'm also a parent.

As a parent, I found most explanations of what children learn were either too technical or too vague to be useful. So I wrote the guide I wished existed: plain English, with no assumptions about what you already know.

The goal isn't to turn you into a programmer. It's to help you understand what your child is learning, clearly and practically, so you can support them with confidence.
