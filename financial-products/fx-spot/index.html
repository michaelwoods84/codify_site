FX Spot

Today's rate, today's money

Two currencies change hands at today's rate, settled almost immediately. Every other FX trade in this course is a variation on this one.

1

What it actually is

An FX spot trade is a straight swap of one currency for another at today's exchange rate, settled almost immediately — typically two business days later, known as "T+2".

No forward-looking commitment, no notional trick — the full amounts on both sides actually move.

2

Why someone would do this

A US company paying a European supplier needs euros, so it sells dollars and buys euros spot. A trader who thinks the euro is about to strengthen buys it outright.

It's the simplest way to convert value from one currency into another, right now.

3

Who's actually doing this

It's the largest, most liquid market in the world by a wide margin — importers and exporters converting real trade flows, travellers changing cash, and trading desks moving trillions of dollars a day.

Almost every other FX product in this course — forwards, swaps — is built from this trade plus a date.

Settlement: T+2 Rate: today's spot rate Full amounts exchanged Largest market in the world by volume
