Privacy notice

Your privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The only information we hold about you is what you choose to send us, such as an email or course feedback, and what you give Gumroad if you buy a course.

Who we are

Codifyit (codifyit.co.uk) publishes practical courses in code, data and finance. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us about anything on this page at hello@codifyit.co.uk.

What we collect, and why

  • Emails you send us. If you email us, for example to register interest or ask a question, we keep your email address and message so we can reply and, if you asked, let you know when a course launches. We rely on our legitimate interest in answering you, or on your request to be kept informed. We delete them when they're no longer needed or when you ask.
  • Purchases. Courses are sold through Gumroad, which handles payment and holds your purchase details under its own privacy policy. Gumroad shares your name, email and order details with us so we can deliver the course and help if something goes wrong. We keep these records for as long as UK tax rules require.
  • Course feedback. If you fill in our feedback form, we receive your answers and, only if you give them, a name and email address. The form is run by our host, Netlify, which stores the responses for us. We use them to improve our courses and, only if you tick that you're happy to be quoted, to show a quote on our website with the name you choose or anonymously. We delete responses after 12 months, or sooner if you ask.
  • Licence checks. When you unlock a course, your licence key is sent to our server, which asks Gumroad whether it's valid. We don't store the key on our server.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies. The interactive parents' course saves two small items in your browser's local storage, only so that it works:

  • codifyit-course-progress-v1: which modules and quizzes you've completed. This never leaves your device.
  • codifyit-course-key-v2: your licence key, so you don't have to type it in again. It's sent to our licence check (described above) when you open the course, to confirm it's still valid.

They're strictly necessary for a service you've asked for, so they don't need your consent. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, though you'll then lose your progress and need to enter your key again.

Fonts and third parties

Our fonts are hosted on this site, so pages make no requests to Google Fonts or other font services. Pages don't embed third-party scripts or social media widgets. Links to Gumroad take you to Gumroad's own site.

Course videos are played by Bunny.net's video player, embedded in the course pages. When you play a video, Bunny.net receives technical information such as your IP address so it can stream the video to you, under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes technical information such as your IP address when it serves a page, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use this to identify you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email hello@codifyit.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, for example by adding analytics, we'll update this page and the date at the top first.
