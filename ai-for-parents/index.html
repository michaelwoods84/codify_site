Who this is for

Made for parents.
Not tech enthusiasts.

You've heard a lot about AI and you want to understand it properly — not to become an expert, but to use it sensibly yourself and help your child do the same. This guide gets you there with real family examples.

You'll get a lot from this if you…

  • Have heard a lot about AI but haven’t used it much yourself
  • Worry about AI and homework, privacy, or fake images
  • Want practical rules your family can actually agree on
  • Want to help your child use AI well, not just ban it

This probably isn't for you if…

  • You already use AI tools daily and confidently
  • You want a technical explanation of how AI models are built
  • You’re looking for workplace AI training

Common questions

FAQ

I’ve never used ChatGPT. Is this for me?

Yes — Module 1 starts from scratch, and you’ll have your first useful conversations with a chatbot by the end of it.

Which AI tools does it cover?

It’s deliberately tool-neutral. Everything works with any mainstream chatbot — ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude or whatever your child’s school uses.

Will it go out of date?

The habits it teaches — protect your details, ask well, check the answer, think before sharing — don’t date. Specific settings and product details will be kept up to date.

How is it different from the parents’ guide?

“What Is My Child Actually Learning?” explains AI from your child’s side. This course is about you using AI tools yourself — and helping your child use them well.
